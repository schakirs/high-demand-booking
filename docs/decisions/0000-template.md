# NNNN — Short title of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Accepted, amended by NNNN | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related**: rules (R…), other decisions (D…); "Amends NNNN" or "Supersedes NNNN" when this
  ADR changes an earlier one

<!-- Once accepted, an ADR is not rewritten. A change is a new ADR that amends (partial
change) or supersedes (full replacement) the earlier one; only the earlier one's Status line
is updated to point to it. -->

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces and constraints are at play?
Include the numbers that matter (load, latency, cost).

## Options considered

<!-- Optional. Omit this section when no real alternative existed (e.g. a project goal or a
principle). In that case, end the Context with one sentence saying why there was no choice. -->

1. **Option A** — how it works; pros; cons.
2. **Option B** — how it works; pros; cons.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

- What we gain.
- What we accept to lose (the trade-off).
- What becomes harder later.

## Revisit when

The measurable signal that would make us reconsider this decision.
